# Strategy Builder

Current V1 item. Started 14 Oct 2026. See [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## Purpose

Flex become a strategy the user builds from conditions. One playbook owns one contract. Direction is Long, Short, or Both when the exchange can hold both sides. The app places the first order, later adds, and the exit. Signals only arm. DCA and Cash and Carry move onto that same shape after the Flex bot does. This replaces the parked position builder / scale-in desk.

## Status

In progress. First step is the entry stack on Flex.

## How the entry stack works

1. The Condition Type stays Price cross, Indicator, Trend, or Signal webhook. It is condition 1 and has no joiner.
2. **Add Condition** appends another Condition Type inside the group: Price cross, Indicator, Trend, or Signal webhook, with the same fields as the Condition Type. **Add Condition Group** starts the next group. That group's first dropdown is Condition Type and opens on Select Condition Type, with nothing selected. And rows inside a group use Condition Type and open on Select Condition Type. On Flex those added Condition Type dropdowns are optional. Leaving one unselected does not block Save, and that row is not stored. Choosing a type makes that row's fields required. DCA added rows stay required.
3. And stays in the current group. Or starts a new group. Groups are OR'd. Conditions inside a group are AND'd. A dropdown choice that cannot be true in that group is disabled: the same webhook twice, two webhooks joined with And, or two tests of one series that cannot be true together. A second price row stays available. At or above A and at or below B on the same price source are disabled only when A is higher than B. Opposite crosses, opposite sits, and a cross against the other side of the same MACD, RSI, moving average, Bollinger band, or Supertrend are disabled. And itself is disabled when that join would create one of those. Cap is 12 conditions and 4 timeframes, including the trigger. A saved Secondary Entry still opens as its filter row.
4. A level that stays true still fires once, on the edge, the same way the bot fires today.
5. One And filter is still stored on the old confirm columns so an older reader sees the same Secondary Entry. Any other stack is `entry_expr` on `futures_automation_rules`. Templates and backtests store that stack on the recipe when the confirm column cannot hold it.
6. Close long / Close short do not take the stack. Those choices stay only on a bot that already closes.
7. Direction matches DCA: Long, Short, or Both. Both is hidden on a one-way exchange. Both keeps the long leg on the existing columns and the short leg in `short_entry`. Each side has its own section, trigger, and stack. Switching Long or Short to Both does not copy or flip the other leg. The new section starts empty until it is configured. Switching Both back to one side keeps that side. The same webhook may arm both sides. Conflict rules stay inside one section. New Backtest and replay Modify open that saved bot with Direction Both and every condition on each builder.
8. On an open Flex bot, Position Sizing sits at the bottom of each entry group, after Add Condition, and stays hidden until that group's Condition Type is selected. Group 1 then shows Size, Unit, and Order. Order is Market or Limit, and Limit shows Limit price. Group 1 is the saved order. Group 2 and later open with Same as Group 1 checked, so they use that saved order and hide their own fields. Clearing the box copies Group 1 into that group and shows its Size, Unit, and Order. Once those fields are visible they are required, including Limit price when Order is Limit. A group that still uses Same as Group 1 does not require its own fields. Those later groups stay on the form and are not saved yet. Direction Both shares that saved order across both entry sections, and only the section that owns the saved columns posts those fields. On DCA, Size unit and Order size sit in that same place, with the same Same as Group 1 box on group 2 and later. Those visible DCA fields are required the same way. When Max orders and Max value are both set, Group 1 shows the calculated order size. Close long and Close short keep Qty to close in Position Sizing. Cash and Carry Position Sizing is unchanged.
9. DCA uses that same stack on Long Entry Condition Builder and Short Entry Condition Builder. Each side has its own Condition Type. One And filter still writes `confirm_*` / `short_confirm_*`. Any other stack is `entry_expr` / `short_entry_expr`. Cash and Carry is unchanged. Saved bot, backtest, and template lists title these sections Long Entry Conditions and Short Entry Conditions. When a side has added conditions, those lists show Group cards and And / Or joiners, the same way as the form. The trigger is the first condition in Group 1. And stays in that group. Or opens the next group. Exit groups use that same Group card. Condition Type there groups Common and Advanced. A side with no added conditions stays a label and value list. New Backtest and replay Modify open each side's Condition Type and added conditions. `/admin/templates` has a Condition list tab with these shapes.

## How the exit stack works

1. Each open side has its own section: Long Exit Condition Builder or Short Exit Condition Builder. Direction Long shows the long section, Short shows the short section, and Both shows both. Close long and Close short hide them. Cash and Carry keeps its own Exit Conditions card. Saved bot, backtest, and template lists title the Flex and DCA sections Long Exit Conditions and Short Exit Conditions.
2. Each card is an action: Take Profit, Trailing Stop, Stop Loss, or Close. **Add action** starts the next card, joined with Or. **Add Condition** inside a card joins with And. Another exit is another action, not a condition group. Enable when includes the price rule as a condition type: Take Profit is Target Hit, Stop Loss is Stop Hit, and Trailing Stop is Trail. Those types hold the basis, method, target, order, stop, and trail fields. A later card of the same action shows that same set, and those fields stay on that action. The same group can add a confirming condition such as RSI. Close uses that stack as the fire rule, unless its scope is fills in profit and the stack is empty. Applies To is Entire Position, Percentage of Position, Quantity, Fills in profit, or Remainder. One remainder per side. Cap is 12 conditions and 4 timeframes inside one action. An unfinished card blocks Save with a specific message. Each leg stores its own cards. Switching to Both does not copy a card onto the other section. DCA and Flex keep their own price fields. Move Breakeven stays on Stop Hit.
3. A side that is still at most one take profit, one trailing stop, and one stop loss, each with only its price condition and scope All, plus at most one Close with scope All, saves today's columns and stores an empty action list. Anything else saves the action list. Opening a saved bot maps the columns back to cards with scope All and that price condition selected, and maps several advanced Or groups to one Close. The same conflict rules as entries apply inside a card.
4. Entry and custom exit share one Indicator list: RSI, MACD, Price vs SMA, Price vs EMA, SMA Cross, EMA Cross, Price vs BB, and ATR band. Trend is Supertrend. Confirm keeps that list, then Supertrend.
5. When a side has an action list, that list is the only exit the worker runs for the side. A price action fires when its price rule is hit and every other condition in that group is true on the same check. A group with no price rule can fire on its own conditions. A trailing stop's peak starts at the reference price on the first active check and does not fire on that check. An activation price is a second gate. Percent is a share of size still unclaimed on that check, so 50% then 50% is half and then a quarter. Remainder always runs last. A stop with scope All or a fill filter reserves those lots before another action takes them. Each firing card is one reduce, floored to the instrument step. Paper writes the reduce. Live flattens only when the claims cover the whole position. A fill filter compares each open fill's price with the mark. One position row per symbol and side stays. A webhook that is only an And peer does not fire the card by itself.
6. The compatible subset still writes the existing columns, and the action field is cleared. Direction Both writes the short classic set to `dca_playbooks.short_classic` or `short_entry.classic`. A richer side writes `exit_actions` on the playbook or Flex rule. DCA Both writes the short list to `short_exit_actions`. Flex Both writes the short list on `short_entry.actions`. The columns stay so the first card's price fields still load. The worker skips those columns when the list is set, and DCA sync clears the copied position take profit, stop, and trailing stop. `futures_positions.exit_runtime` stores the arm. A classic price, percent, retracement, activation price, limit, or breakeven activation that is still empty cannot be saved. The same check covers the other required fields.

## How Flex additional orders work

1. DCA Additional Orders stays Additional Order Types and Additional Order Scaling. That model does not change.
2. Flex uses Long Additional Orders Condition Builder and Short Additional Orders Condition Builder, after the entry builders, when the bot is not closing. Direction Long shows the long section, Short shows the short section, and Both shows both. Each side keeps its own groups. Switching to Both does not copy one side onto the other. Each section uses the same group cards as the entry builder. Group 1 opens on Condition Type (Select Condition Type), with no required mark. Leaving it unselected does not block Save. The options match the entry selector: Indicator, Trend, Price Cross, and Signal Webhook. Selecting one shows that entry condition's fields. **Add Condition** appends a Condition Type inside the group. **Add Condition Group** starts the next group, joined with Or, and that group's first dropdown is Condition Type. The same conflict rules as entries apply inside a group. Cap is 12 conditions.
3. Position Sizing sits at the bottom of each group and stays hidden until that group's Condition Type is selected. Group 1 then shows Size, Unit, and Order. Group 2 and later open with Same as Group 1 checked and show those fields only after it is cleared. Clearing the box copies Group 1 into that group. Order is Market or Limit. Limit shows Limit price. Once those fields are visible they are required, including Limit price when Order is Limit. A group that still uses Same as Group 1 does not require its own fields. Editing a condition or that group's order marks the bot unsaved. Save stays off while a visible Position Sizing field is empty. These fields are not saved yet, so parameter lists and the saved bot configuration show Condition Type, Size, Unit, and Order as Off. Those lists title the sections Long Additional Orders and Short Additional Orders. A successful Save clears the unsaved notice. Close long and Close short omit both steps.
