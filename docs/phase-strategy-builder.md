# Strategy Builder

Started 14 Oct 2026. Click moved to the full system test (V1 item 8) on 21 Oct 2026. See [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## Purpose

Flex become a strategy the user builds from conditions. One playbook owns one contract. Direction is Long, Short, or Both when the exchange can hold both sides. The app places the first order, later adds, and the exit. Signals only arm. DCA and Cash and Carry move onto that same shape after the Flex bot does. This replaces the parked position builder / scale-in desk.

## Condition groups

Specified. Not built yet. The form, the worker, and replay change together. This replaces the flat Condition Type list (Price cross, Retrace, Indicator, Trend, Signal webhook, Position protection).

The first dropdown is the group. The next dropdown is the condition in that group. Signal webhook stays its own Condition Type. It is not inside a group.

- **Indicator.** Supertrend, RSI, RSI divergence, MACD, Price vs SMA, Price vs EMA, SMA Cross, EMA Cross, Price vs BB, ATR band. There is no Trend group. Supertrend keeps the fields it has today (ATR period, multiplier, When, timeframe). RSI divergence stays an Indicator kind, next to RSI, not its own Condition Type.
- **Price Action.** Price Cross, Retrace. Entry, exit, and DCA start Retrace keep the candle-window fields. Flex additional-order Retrace keeps the position test: From is High, Low, Initial Entry Price, or Average Entry Price, with no Lookback and no Timeframe.
- **Position State.** Capital Protection Status, Unrealized P&L Status. Flex additional orders only, and only on **Add Condition**. It is a check on the open position, not a trigger. It is not the first condition of a group. It is not on entry, exit, Close, or DCA.

Capital Protection Status is the condition that was Position protection. Fields stay Position (this bot's open position on this contract and side), Is (Protected or Protected - No Loss), and Amount (Entire position or At least %). Protected uses `provenPct` and counts a stop even when that price would close at a loss. Protected - No Loss uses `noLoss.provenPct`: the same stops, but only when the stop price is at or better than the average entry. A long stop qualifies at or above entry. A short stop qualifies at or below entry. A price equal to entry qualifies. At least % is required when Amount is At least. It reads the largest single qualifying stop, not the stacked sum. It counts a resting stop, a trailing stop that is tracking after Activation %, and a breakeven stop after it has latched. It does not count take profit, Close, a waiting trail, or a breakeven that has not moved. A flat position is unmet, so the add does not fire. The worker, replay, and the replay chart read that book the same way. Replay stores it on the fill. A backtest with no protective stop never sees either value as true. A saved Not protected condition does not load.

Unrealized P&L Status checks that same position. Is is **Position in Profit** or **Position in Loss**. Position in Profit is unrealized P&L above zero. Position in Loss is unrealized P&L below zero. Zero is neither, so it is unmet. A flat position is unmet. No new columns. It is stored in the additional-order expression with the other leaves.

A saved Trend condition opens as Indicator, Supertrend. A saved Position protection condition opens as Position State, Capital Protection Status. A saved group that already starts on that condition still shows it. A new group cannot start on Position State.

Replay's chart toolbar gains a **Show Position States** checkbox immediately after Show Enabled Exits. It starts unchecked. Checking it shows a small overlay on the chart. The overlay lists four rows for each side that is open at the current replay bar. A flat side is left out. Both sides open shows Long, then Short. It reads the exit book already stored for that bar, and the unrealized P&L of that open side. It does not draw prices, and it does not change Show Enabled Exits.

- **Capital Protected.** True or false, and `provenPct`. True when that percent is above zero. False shows 0%.
- **Capital Protected from Loss.** True or false, and `noLoss.provenPct`. True when that percent is above zero. False shows 0%.
- **In Profit.** True or false. True when unrealized P&L is above zero. This is Unrealized P&L Status, Position in Profit.
- **In Loss.** True or false. True when unrealized P&L is below zero. This is Position in Loss. Zero is false for both.

### Engine and replay

The form, save, the worker, replay, and the lists all use one set of leaves. A condition the form can save is a condition the worker and replay both evaluate. A condition the form cannot save does not fire if it is already stored.

Supertrend, Price Cross, and Retrace keep the leaves they have today (`trend`, `price`, `retrace`). Save still writes those leaves. The worker, replay, backtest, and chart series keep the evaluators they have, including the Flex additional-order retrace that measures from the open position and stores its arm on `add_retrace` and `short_add_retrace`. The form is what changes: Supertrend is chosen under Indicator, and Price Cross and Retrace are chosen under Price Action. A saved Trend bot opens as Indicator, Supertrend, and still starts on the same bar in replay and on the same tick in the worker. DCA entry and Flex entry both do this. Exit Add Condition and Close do this too. Confirm keeps the Supertrend filter it has.

Capital Protection Status keeps the `protection` leaf. The worker and replay already pass `provenPct` and `noLoss.provenPct` into that leaf on a Flex add. That path stays. The lists say Capital Protection Status instead of Position protection. The chart log keeps the sentence it has, such as Protected - No Loss, entire position.

Unrealized P&L Status is a new leaf on the Flex additional-order expression only (`entry_expr.add`, and `short_entry.add` for Both). No new column. Is is Position in Profit or Position in Loss. One function decides it, and the worker, replay, and the overlay all call it. A long is (mark − average entry) × open qty. A short is (average entry − mark) × open qty. Position in Profit is that number above zero. Position in Loss is that number below zero. Zero is unmet. A flat position is unmet. The worker passes the tick's mark and that side's average entry and open qty. Replay passes the bar close it already uses for that position's unrealized P&L. Each side is separate. The long add does not read the short position.

The Flex add stays a rising edge. The group fires when every And leaf in one Or group is true and the group was not true on the previous check. Position State is an And leaf in that check. It does not fire the add by itself, and it does not fire on the same check as the opening entry. Paper and live use that same check. The browser does not decide it.

Save rejects Position State on an entry, an exit, Close, DCA, and as the first condition of a new additional-order group. Replay and the worker treat a stored Position State leaf in those places as unmet, so an old or hand-written row cannot fire there. A saved additional-order group that already starts on Position protection still loads, still shows, and still evaluates. A new group cannot start on it.

Show Position States reads the replay position at the current bar through those same functions. Capital Protected is `provenPct` from the exit book the add already reads. Capital Protected from Loss is `noLoss.provenPct`. In Profit and In Loss are the unrealized function above. Playing and scrubbing use the bar on screen. The checkbox does not place orders and does not change Show Enabled Exits. The live desk chart does not gain this overlay.

Saved bot, backtest, and template lists, New Backtest, replay Modify, and the admin Condition list show the group and the condition. Supertrend appears as Indicator. Capital Protection Status and Unrealized P&L Status appear as Position State, with Is and Amount. An add that fires writes that sentence in the position log.

Tests lock the pair. A saved Supertrend start still fires in the worker and in replay. Price Cross and Retrace still fire there. A Flex add with Protected, and one with Protected - No Loss, still fires and still stays quiet when the book is short of the amount. A Flex add with Position in Profit fires only when unrealized P&L is above zero, and Position in Loss only when it is below zero, in the worker and on the same rule in replay, for a long, a short, and Both. Zero and a flat position do not fire. An entry or exit that contains Position State does not save. On a replay bar, the four overlay rows match the two coverage percents and the two P&L booleans for each open side.

## Retrace, RSI divergence, and Position State

No new columns. Lead Retrace and lead RSI divergence extras live on `entry_expr` / `short_entry_expr` (`retrace`, `divergence`). Added rows, exit cards, and Flex additional-order conditions use the existing expression JSON. `entry_source`, DCA `start_kind`, and the indicator kind checks allow `retrace` and `rsi_divergence`. A saved `trend` start opens as Indicator, Supertrend. Capital Protection Status keeps the `protection` leaf. Unrealized P&L Status is a new leaf on the additional-order expression only.

Entry, exit, and DCA start retrace fields are Price source (Last, Mark, or Index, default Last), From (High on a long, Low on a short), Retrace % (required), Lookback (20 candles), and Timeframe (required, and it counts toward the 4-timeframe cap). The test is at least that percent back from the high, or up from the low, of that candle window. It fires on the edge when the retrace first becomes true, and it can fire again after price recovers. One retrace per source, From, and timeframe inside an And group. On a price exit it has to be true on the same check as the price rule. On Close it can be the fire rule.

Flex additional-order retrace is a different test. It has no Lookback and no Timeframe. From is High, Low, Initial Entry Price, or Average Entry Price. High is the highest price since the first fill of that position, and the add fires when price is the configured percent below it. After the order is placed, that test stays false until price makes a higher high than the high in force when the add fired, and then retraces the percent from that new high. Low is the lowest price since the first fill, and the add fires on a percent bounce up. It stays false until a lower low, then the percent bounce from that new low. A wick to the new extreme and a close back through the percent on the same bar counts. Initial Entry Price is the first fill and does not move when later adds fill. A long fires when price is the percent below it, and a short fires when price is the percent above it. That test fires once for the position. Average Entry Price uses the live average, which updates after each add, with the same long-below and short-above test. It can fire again when the condition is false and then true, which is what happens after the fill moves the average. The arm updates only when the additional order is placed, so a blocked And does not consume it. Price source stays Last, Mark, or Index. One of each From per price source inside an And. An older saved additional-order retrace that still has Lookback and Timeframe is read as this position test. The next save omits those fields. The worker stores the running high, low, and initial-entry arm on `add_retrace` and `short_add_retrace`. Saving the bot, or the position going flat, clears them.

RSI divergence fields are When (Bullish or Bearish, required, default follows the side), Style (Regular), Period (14), Timeframe, Pivot bars (5), Span (50 candles), Min price % (empty means any), and Min RSI points (empty means any). Regular bullish is a lower low in price and a higher low in RSI. Regular bearish is a higher high in price and a lower high in RSI. Hidden bullish is a higher low in price and a lower low in RSI. Hidden bearish is a lower high in price and a higher high in RSI. Pivots are found on price. RSI is read on those bars. It fires once, on the candle that confirms the newest pair, when the minimums pass. A pair that fails a minimum is still drawn. The chart draws every confirmed pair whose swings are at most Span candles apart. A reference study draws regular and hidden, bullish and bearish. A condition draws the pairs for its When and Style. Each pair is a line on price and a line on the RSI pane, with a triangle on the RSI at each swing. Regular bullish is green, hidden bullish is cyan, regular bearish is red, and hidden bearish is amber. An older pair stays when a newer one appears. Swings that still need bars on the right are not drawn. Drawing does not place the order. The opposite When on the same period and timeframe is disabled inside an And. The same When is allowed. On a price exit it shares the check with the price rule. Close can use it as the fire rule.

Capital Protection Status and Unrealized P&L Status are specified under [Condition groups](#condition-groups). Both are secondary conditions on Flex additional orders. Neither can be the first condition of a group. The chart log still uses the protection sentence, such as Protected - No Loss, entire position.
