# Strategy Builder

Current V1 item. Started 14 Oct 2026. See [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## Purpose

Flex become a strategy the user builds from conditions. One playbook owns one contract. Direction is Long, Short, or Both when the exchange can hold both sides. The app places the first order, later adds, and the exit. Signals only arm. DCA and Cash and Carry move onto that same shape after the Flex bot does. This replaces the parked position builder / scale-in desk.

## Status

In progress. First step is the entry stack on Flex.

## How the entry stack works

1. The Condition Type stays Price cross, Retrace, Indicator, Trend, or Signal webhook. It is condition 1 and has no joiner. Retrace sits after Price cross. RSI divergence is an Indicator kind, next to RSI, not its own Condition Type.
2. **Add Condition** appends another Condition Type inside the group: Price cross, Retrace, Indicator, Trend, or Signal webhook, with the same fields as the Condition Type. **Add Condition Group** starts the next group. That group's first dropdown is Condition Type and opens on Select Condition Type, with nothing selected. And rows inside a group use Condition Type and open on Select Condition Type. Those added Condition Type dropdowns are optional on DCA and Flex. Leaving one unselected does not block Save, and that row is not stored. Choosing a type makes that row's fields required. The first condition's price, indicator, trend, and webhook fields use the same five-column row on both desks.
3. And stays in the current group. Or starts a new group. Groups are OR'd. Conditions inside a group are AND'd. A dropdown choice that cannot be true in that group is disabled: the same webhook twice, two webhooks joined with And, or two tests of one series that cannot be true together. A second price row stays available. At or above A and at or below B on the same price source are disabled only when A is higher than B. Opposite crosses, opposite sits, and a cross against the other side of the same MACD, RSI, moving average, Bollinger band, or Supertrend are disabled. And itself is disabled when that join would create one of those. Cap is 12 conditions and 4 timeframes, including the trigger. A saved Secondary Entry still opens as its filter row.
4. A level that stays true still fires once, on the edge, the same way the bot fires today.
5. One And filter is still stored on the old confirm columns so an older reader sees the same Secondary Entry. Any other stack is `entry_expr` on `futures_automation_rules`. Templates and backtests store that stack on the recipe when the confirm column cannot hold it.
6. Close long / Close short do not take the stack. Those choices stay only on a bot that already closes.
7. Direction matches DCA: Long, Short, or Both. Both is hidden on a one-way exchange. Both keeps the long leg on the existing columns and the short leg in `short_entry`. Each side has its own section, trigger, and stack. Switching Long or Short to Both does not copy or flip the other leg. The new section starts empty until it is configured. Switching Both back to one side keeps that side. The same webhook may arm both sides. Conflict rules stay inside one section. New Backtest and replay Modify open that saved bot with Direction Both and every condition on each builder.
8. On an open Flex bot, Position Sizing sits at the bottom of each entry group, after Add Condition, and stays hidden until that group's Condition Type is selected. Group 1 then shows Size, Unit, and Order. Order is Market or Limit, and Limit shows Limit price. Group 1 is the saved order. Group 2 and later open with Same as Group 1 checked, so they use that saved order and hide their own fields. Clearing the box copies Group 1 into that group and shows its Size, Unit, and Order. Once those fields are visible they are required, including Limit price when Order is Limit. A group that still uses Same as Group 1 does not require its own fields. Those later groups stay on the form and are not saved yet. Direction Both shares that saved order across both entry sections, and only the section that owns the saved columns posts those fields. On DCA, Size unit and Order size sit in that same place, with the same Same as Group 1 box on group 2 and later. Those visible DCA fields are required the same way. When Max orders and Max value are both set, Group 1 shows the calculated order size. Close long and Close short keep Qty to close in Position Sizing. Cash and Carry Position Sizing is unchanged.
9. DCA uses that same stack on Long Entry Condition Builder and Short Entry Condition Builder. Each side has its own Condition Type. One And filter still writes `confirm_*` / `short_confirm_*`. Any other stack is `entry_expr` / `short_entry_expr`. Cash and Carry is unchanged. Saved bot, backtest, and template lists title these sections Long Entry Conditions and Short Entry Conditions. When a side has added conditions, those lists show Group cards and And / Or joiners, the same way as the form. The trigger is the first condition in Group 1. And stays in that group. Or opens the next group. Exit groups use that same Group card. Condition Type there groups Common and Advanced. A side with no added conditions stays a label and value list. New Backtest and replay Modify open each side's Condition Type and added conditions. `/admin/templates` has a Condition list tab with these shapes.

## How the exit stack works

1. Each open side has its own section: Long Exit Condition Builder or Short Exit Condition Builder. Direction Long shows the long section, Short shows the short section, and Both shows both. Close long and Close short hide them. Cash and Carry keeps its own Exit Conditions card. Saved bot, backtest, and template lists title the Flex and DCA sections Long Exit Conditions and Short Exit Conditions.
2. Each card is an action: Take Profit, Stop Loss, Breakeven Stop, Trailing Stop, or Close. The card title is Group 1, then Group 2, the same as the entry groups. The helper on entry, additional-order, and exit groups is Fires when all conditions are true. **Add action** starts the next card, joined with Or. **Add Condition** inside a card joins with And. Another exit is another action, not a condition group. Take Profit, Stop Loss, Breakeven Stop, and Trailing Stop open on their price fields. Those fields are the condition, so the card does not show a Condition Type dropdown. Indicator, Trend, Price Cross, Retrace, and Signal Webhook are not choices there. **Add Condition** puts them underneath, and they have to be true on the same check as the price rule. Retrace and RSI divergence can sit in that And. Those fields hold the basis, method, target, order, stop, trail, and breakeven values. A later card of the same action shows that same set, and those fields stay on that action. Close keeps Indicator, Trend, Price Cross, Retrace, and Signal Webhook as Condition Type, and it can fire in profit or at a loss. RSI divergence is an Indicator kind there too. Close can use Retrace or RSI divergence as the fire rule. Close uses that stack as the fire rule, unless its scope is fills in profit and the stack is empty. Applies To sits at the bottom of every card, including Breakeven Stop, after Add Condition. It is Entire Position, Percentage of Position, Quantity, Fills in profit, or Remainder. One remainder per side. Cap is 12 conditions and 4 timeframes inside one action. An unfinished card blocks Save with a specific message. Each leg stores its own cards. Switching to Both does not copy a card onto the other section. DCA and Flex keep their own price fields. Breakeven Stop is its own action. One per side. It has no Condition Type dropdown. Applies To uses the same choices as the other cards. Entire Position still writes the breakeven columns. A smaller scope, or any added condition, writes the action list.
3. A side that is still at most one take profit, one trailing stop, one stop loss, and one breakeven stop, each with only its price condition and scope All, plus at most one Close with scope All, saves today's columns and stores an empty action list. Anything else saves the action list. Opening a saved bot maps the columns back to cards with scope All. The price fields are filled from those columns. Several advanced Or groups map to one Close. The same conflict rules as entries apply inside a card.
4. Entry and custom exit share one Indicator list: RSI, RSI divergence, MACD, Price vs SMA, Price vs EMA, SMA Cross, EMA Cross, Price vs BB, and ATR band. Trend is Supertrend. Confirm keeps that list, then Supertrend. RSI divergence is not a Condition Type.
5. When a side has an action list, that list is the only exit the worker runs for the side. A price action fires when its price rule is hit and every other condition in that group is true on the same check. Close can fire on its own conditions. A Breakeven Stop card does not reduce. Once profit reaches its percent, the worker moves the protective stop to entry plus or minus the offset and latches that the move is done. Entire Position keeps the full stop. A smaller Applies To sets that stop to the sized quantity. A take profit, stop, or trailing stop with no price rule does not fire. A trailing stop's peak starts at the reference price on the first active check and does not fire on that check. A trailing stop on either desk is Activation %, then Trailing %. Activation % is how far the position must be in profit before the trail starts. Trailing % is how far price can come back from the best price. Breakeven Stop is Activation %, then Buffer %. Percent is a share of size still unclaimed on that check, so 50% then 50% is half and then a quarter. Remainder always runs last. A stop with scope All or a fill filter reserves those lots before another action takes them. Each firing card is one reduce, floored to the instrument step. Paper writes the reduce. Live flattens only when the claims cover the whole position. A fill filter compares each open fill's price with the mark. One position row per symbol and side stays. A webhook that is only an And peer does not fire the card by itself.
6. The compatible subset still writes the existing columns, and the action field is cleared. Direction Both writes the short classic set to `dca_playbooks.short_classic` or `short_entry.classic`. A richer side writes `exit_actions` on the playbook or Flex rule. DCA Both writes the short list to `short_exit_actions`. Flex Both writes the short list on `short_entry.actions`. The columns stay so the first card's price fields still load. The worker skips those columns when the list is set, and DCA sync clears the copied position take profit, stop, and trailing stop. `futures_positions.exit_runtime` stores the arm and the last exit book. A classic price, percent, retracement, activation percent, activation price, limit, or breakeven activation that is still empty cannot be saved. A Flex Trailing % is stored on the action list, because the classic columns hold a price distance. A Flex activation percent or a DCA activation price is stored on the action list for the same reason. The same check covers the other required fields.
7. Every check that prices an open position writes that book onto `exit_runtime`. An action list stores the arm and the book together. Classic columns use the same book, one node per column that is set, and the book does not place orders. Each node keeps its status and whether each condition leaf was met. Price levels use the mark, and a trailing stop uses the last. The same check passes the index price, so an Index price cross can be met. A Flex breakeven move is still requested from the last price, and that check is the one marked `moving`. Coverage is stored two ways. `provenQty` is the largest single protecting action, so overlap cannot inflate it. `stackedQty` adds those quantities and stops at the open size. Protective coverage is a stop loss, a trailing stop that is tracking, and a breakeven move after it has latched. The latched size is the Applies To quantity, and Entire Position is the open quantity. The check that requests the breakeven move is `moving` and covers nothing. Remainder does not count as resting cover. Take profit and Close stay in the tree and add no coverage. `trailingOrBreakeven` is the pair a later condition can read. Replay fill events store the same book on `exitBook`. A custom exit expression is not an exit-action node. Flex additional orders can read that protective coverage. The position and replay display of the book are not in this step. An ATR take profit is included once the synced position price exists.

## How additional orders work

1. DCA Additional Orders stays Additional Order Types and Additional Order Scaling. Averaging, spacing, the order-size multiplier, and max orders stay. An optional later step, Additional order conditions, can require Retrace or an Indicator (including RSI divergence) before the next clip. Those gates do not replace the ladder. An empty step leaves the ladder as it is. The conditions are stored on `entry_expr.add` and `short_entry_expr.add`.
2. Flex uses Long Additional Orders Condition Builder and Short Additional Orders Condition Builder, after the entry builders, when the bot is not closing. Direction Long shows the long section, Short shows the short section, and Both shows both. Each side keeps its own groups. Switching to Both does not copy one side onto the other. Each section uses the same group cards as the entry builder. Group 1 opens on Condition Type (Select Condition Type), with no required mark. Leaving it unselected does not block Save. The options are Indicator, Trend, Price Cross, Retrace, Signal Webhook, and Position protection. RSI divergence is an Indicator kind. Position protection is this Flex section only. It is not on entries, exits, or DCA. Selecting one shows that condition's fields. **Add Condition** appends a Condition Type inside the group. **Add Condition Group** starts the next group, joined with Or, and that group's first dropdown is Condition Type. The same conflict rules as entries apply inside a group. Cap is 12 conditions. The conditions are stored on `entry_expr.add` and, for Both, `short_entry.add`.
3. Position Sizing sits at the bottom of each Flex group and stays hidden until that group's Condition Type is selected. Group 1 then shows Size, Unit, and Order. Group 2 and later open with Same as Group 1 checked and show those fields only after it is cleared. Clearing the box copies Group 1 into that group. Order is Market or Limit. Limit shows Limit price. Once those fields are visible they are required, including Limit price when Order is Limit. A group that still uses Same as Group 1 does not require its own fields. Editing a condition or that group's order marks the bot unsaved. Save stays off while a visible Position Sizing field is empty. Size, Unit, and Order are not saved yet, and Flex does not place the add. Parameter lists title the sections Long Additional Orders and Short Additional Orders. A successful Save clears the unsaved notice. Close long and Close short omit both steps.

## Retrace, RSI divergence, and position protection

No new columns. Lead Retrace and lead RSI divergence extras live on `entry_expr` / `short_entry_expr` (`retrace`, `divergence`). Added rows, exit cards, and additional-order conditions use the existing expression JSON. `entry_source`, DCA `start_kind`, and the indicator kind checks allow `retrace` and `rsi_divergence`.

Retrace fields are Price source (Last, Mark, or Index, default Last), From (High on a long, Low on a short), Retrace % (required), Lookback (20 candles), and Timeframe (required, and it counts toward the 4-timeframe cap). The test is at least that percent. It fires on the edge when the retrace first becomes true, and it can fire again after price recovers. One retrace per source, From, and timeframe inside an And group. On a price exit it has to be true on the same check as the price rule. On Close it can be the fire rule.

RSI divergence fields are When (Bullish or Bearish, required, default follows the side), Style (Regular), Period (14), Timeframe, Pivot bars (5), Span (50 candles), Min price % (empty means any), and Min RSI points (empty means any). Regular bullish is a lower low in price and a higher low in RSI. Regular bearish is a higher high in price and a lower high in RSI. Hidden bullish is a higher low in price and a lower low in RSI. Hidden bearish is a lower high in price and a higher high in RSI. Pivots are found on price. RSI is read on those bars. It fires once, on the candle that confirms the second swing, when the minimums pass. A pair that fails a minimum is still drawn. The chart draws one price segment and one RSI segment between the two confirmed swings. A newer pair replaces the line. Swings that still need bars on the right are not drawn. Drawing does not place the order. The opposite When on the same period and timeframe is disabled inside an And. The same When is allowed. On a price exit it shares the check with the price rule. Close can use it as the fire rule.

Position protection reads the exit book's largest single protective stop (`provenPct`), not the stacked sum. It counts a resting stop, a trailing stop that is tracking after Activation %, and a breakeven stop after it has latched. It does not count take profit, Close, a waiting trail, or a breakeven that has not moved. Fields are Position (this bot's open position on this contract and side), Is (Protected or Not protected), and Amount (Entire position or At least %). At least % is required when Amount is At least. A flat position is unmet, so the add does not fire. Replay uses the book already stored on fills. A backtest with no protective stop never sees Protected as true.

## Plan

Position Builder is a plan row under Max Bots. Admin can include it or give it a free trial. While the trial is open, Add Condition, Add Condition Group, and Add action work, and a bot saved then keeps that structure. After the trial, those buttons stay visible and the click shows the trial-ended gate. The conditions already on that bot stay editable. A plan that never included it uses the not-allowed gate on the same clicks. See [phase-entitlements.md](phase-entitlements.md).
